# Authorship criteria — prompt library resource paper

** This file is published before contributions are solicited, and applied without exception**

Anyone meeting a threshold below is an author on the resource paper describing
the library. Everyone who contributes anything at all is named in
`CONTRIBUTORS.md` and in the Zenodo record.

## Thresholds

Meet **any** of the following:

1. **Three accepted entries** (score ≥ 15, Documentation ≥ 3).
2. **Two accepted entries plus five completed peer reviews** using the scoring sheet.
3. **Five documented failure modes**, each with model version, date, and detection
   method, contributed as entries or as issues against existing entries.
4. **Substantial infrastructure or coordination work**: leading a domain working
   session, maintaining the repository, or building the re-validation tooling.
5. **Drafting a section of the manuscript**, on invitation from the writing group.

## Author order

- Alphabetical within contribution tiers, except for the first three and last two
  positions, which go to the writing group and the project leads.
- The contribution list is public in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`.

## Ground rules

- Every author must approve the final manuscript and be able to explain the
  library's design. Anyone who unreachable at submission is moved to acknowledgements.
- Attendance at meetings is not a contribution. Presence at the hackathon is not
  a contribution.
- Early-career contributors are eligible on the same terms as PIs.
- Disputes shall go to the CoP chair and secretary jointly. Unresolved cases will 
  escalate per the CoP Terms of Reference.

## AI use declaration

Prompts and outputs in this library are by definition AI-assisted. Every entry
records the model and version used. The manuscript will carry a full AI-use
statement describing which parts were model-generated and what human validation
followed.
